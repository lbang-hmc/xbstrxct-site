---
title: Ready-to-hand
description: "Heidegger's term for equipment that has withdrawn into use, noticed only when it breaks."
tags: [phenomenology, embodiment]
---

Heidegger's term (*zuhanden*) for equipment that has withdrawn into use: the hammer you are hammering with, the keyboard you are typing on. When the tool breaks or fails, it becomes *present-at-hand* (*vorhanden*), an object to be noticed and inspected. Most good abstractions aim to stay ready-to-hand.
